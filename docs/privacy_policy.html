Privacy Policy — Open & Shut

Effective Date: November 27, 2026
Package: com.icanbenchurcat.voicenotes

1. Local-Only Guarantee

Open & Shut processes and stores all voice recordings, photos, transcripts, summaries, tasks, and tags strictly on your device.

  • No Internet Permission: The release app does not declare or inherit android.permission.INTERNET.
  • No Accounts or Servers: No sign-in, no cloud sync, and no backend servers.
  • No Analytics or Ads: Zero analytics, crash-upload SDKs, or advertising trackers.

2. Permissions Used

  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO) & Microphone Foreground Service: Used only when you start a recording so capture continues reliably if you switch apps or lock your screen.
  • Notifications: Displays active recording controls (Pause, Resume, Stop, Cancel) and local sorting completion updates.

Photo notes use Android's system Photo Picker and Camera delegation without requesting CAMERA or READ_MEDIA_IMAGES.

3. Data Retention & Deletion

All notes and media remain in app-private storage excluded from cloud backups. You can delete individual notes or purge all data anytime under Offline AI & Storage, or enable optional 90-day audio cleanup.
